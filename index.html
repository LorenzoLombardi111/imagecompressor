Is freeimagecompressor.app private and safe to use?

freeimagecompressor.app processes images entirely within your browser using the Canvas API. When you drop an image, it is read into your browser's memory, drawn onto an invisible canvas element, and re-exported at the specified quality level. At no point is any image data transmitted over the network.

There are zero server uploads, zero analytics scripts, zero tracking cookies, and zero third-party requests involved in the compression process. Your images exist only in your browser's memory for the duration of the session and are released when you close the tab or clear them from the tool.

The Canvas API also naturally strips EXIF metadata — including GPS coordinates, camera model, timestamps, and other embedded data — during re-encoding. This provides an additional layer of privacy protection by default.

Is freeimagecompressor.app really free?

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Your images never leave your device. All compression happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. There are no server uploads, no cloud processing, and no data transmission of any kind.

What image formats are supported?

freeimagecompressor.app supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF images. You can also convert between formats — for example, converting PNG to JPG or any format to WebP for better compression.

How much can I reduce my image file size?

File size reduction depends on the image and settings. JPG images typically see 40-80% reduction at quality 80%. PNG files see modest reduction unless converted to JPG or WebP. WebP format generally produces the smallest files with good quality.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Yes. You can compress up to 20 images at once using batch compression. Drop or select multiple files, set your quality and format preferences, and click Compress All. You can download all results as a single ZIP file.

Does compression reduce image quality?

Lossy compression (JPG, WebP) does reduce quality slightly, but at 80% quality the difference is usually imperceptible. You can use the live preview and comparison slider to check quality before downloading. PNG compression is lossless.
